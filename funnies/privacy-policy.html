Funnies Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026. Funnies is made by Bramble Apps (Ruth McCarthy), Ireland. Questions: funnies@brambleapps.com

The short version

Funnies is made for children, so we built it to collect nothing at all. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind.

Where your photos go

Photos are taken on your device and saved only to your device's photo library, and only when you tap Save. They are never uploaded anywhere. Funnies does not connect to the internet and works fully in airplane mode.

Face detection and face data

Some filters place ears, hats and other props on faces. To place them, Funnies uses on-device face detection (Google ML Kit's on-device library) to find the position of a face and of features such as the eyes, nose and mouth in the live camera picture.

What is collected: nothing. Those positions are worked out on your device and held only in the phone's working memory for the fraction of a second that each camera frame is on screen, then replaced by the next frame. They are never saved to a file, never embedded in your photos, and never leave the device.

Use: face positions are used for one purpose only: drawing the props in the right place on the live picture. They are not used to identify anyone, and no biometric template is ever created.

Storage and retention: face data is not stored at all. It exists in memory for less than a second and is gone when the frame passes, and entirely when you leave the camera or close the app. The retention period is zero.

Sharing and third parties: face data is never shared with anyone, including us. The detection library runs entirely on the device and makes no network connections; Funnies makes no network connections at all.

Deletion: because nothing is ever stored, there is nothing to delete. Photos you choose to save are ordinary pictures in your photo library with no face data attached, and you can delete them there at any time.

Purchases

Filter packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see names, emails or card details. Purchases sit behind a parental gate so only a grown-up can buy.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect no personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here and inside an app update, described in the update notes.
