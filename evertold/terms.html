What Evertold is

Evertold is an app for capturing family stories, one gentle question at a time, and turning them into a keepsake book.

Your content

Everything your family puts into Evertold belongs to your family. We claim no rights over your stories, photos or recordings. You are responsible for having the storyteller's blessing to record and share their memories within your family.

The free plan

The free plan is genuinely free: one storyteller, unlimited questions you write yourself, typed answers, photos, and the book export.

Subscriptions

Mid and Pro are auto-renewing subscriptions billed through your Apple ID. They renew until cancelled, which you can do any time in your App Store settings; prices and periods are shown in the app before any purchase. AI-crafted questions include a monthly allowance per person (currently 100); after that, questions come from Evertold's built-in library until the month turns. Apple's standard End User Licence Agreement applies.

Care and limits

We work hard to keep your stories safe: written stories sync to your family's private cloud space, and the app lets you export everything whenever you like. Still, Evertold is provided as is, and you should keep your own copies of anything irreplaceable. Photos and voice recordings currently live only on the phone that made them, so mind that phone.

Fair use

Don't use Evertold to store unlawful content or to harass anyone, and don't attempt to access another family's data. We may suspend access that abuses the service or its AI features.
