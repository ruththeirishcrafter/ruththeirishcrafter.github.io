Funnies Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 July 2026. Funnies is made by Bramble Apps (Ruth McCarthy), Ireland. Questions: funnies@brambleapps.com

The short version

Funnies is made for children, so we built it to collect nothing at all. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind.

Where your photos go

Photos are taken on your device and saved only to your device's photo library, and only when you tap Save. They are never uploaded anywhere. Funnies does not connect to the internet and works fully in airplane mode.

Face detection

Some filters place ears or hats on faces. That face detection happens entirely on your device. No images or face data ever leave the phone, and nothing is stored after the moment passes.

Purchases

Filter packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see names, emails or card details. Purchases sit behind a parental gate so only a grown-up can buy.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect no personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here and inside an app update, described in the update notes.
