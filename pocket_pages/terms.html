Pocket Pages — Terms of Use

Last updated: July 2026

The deal

Pocket Pages is a companion for your paper planner: photograph your pages and keep pastel sticky notes, all on your device. Everyone gets a free month of the full app. After that the free version holds up to 10 sticky notes and 3 planner pages, and Pocket Pages Pro removes the limits.

The free trial and Pro

The free month needs no card and does not turn into a subscription; when it ends the app simply settles into the free limits until you choose to unlock. Pro is available as a one-off lifetime unlock, or as a monthly or yearly plan. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your device Settings at least 24 hours before the period ends. Payment goes through your Apple account, and Apple's standard End User Licence Agreement applies.

Your content

Your notes and photos are yours. They stay on your device; we never see them and claim no rights over them.

Sensible limits

The app is provided as is, without warranties. It is a notebook, not a safety-critical system: please do not rely on it as the only copy of information you cannot afford to lose, and keep your device backups switched on.
