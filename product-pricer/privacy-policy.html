Product Pricer privacy policy

Last updated 4 July 2026. Product Pricer is made by Ruth McCarthy, Ireland. Questions: ruth@theirishcrafter.com

The short version

Everything you put into Product Pricer stays on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and we cannot see your products, prices or photos.

What the app stores

Your products, materials, photos, fee presets and settings are saved only on your device. Photos you add are copied into the app's own storage on the phone. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Purchases

Product Pricer Pro purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is active; this involves an anonymous device identifier and receipt data, never your name, email or contacts. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple and never reach us.

Permissions

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a product photo, and the images stay on your device.

Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the change noted in the app update notes.
