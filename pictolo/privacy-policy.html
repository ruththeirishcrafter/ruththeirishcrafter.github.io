Pictolo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Pictolo is built to know as little about you as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. Pictolo has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your puzzle progress, streak and settings are stored only on your device. We never see them.

Purchases

If you buy Pictolo Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. To unlock Pro and restore purchases, the app checks your purchase receipt through RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. This uses an anonymous identifier, contains no personal details, and is not used for tracking.

What leaves your phone

Nothing else. Pictolo works fully offline.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device, so "Start fresh" in Settings, or deleting the app, removes it all.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here and in the app before it takes effect.
