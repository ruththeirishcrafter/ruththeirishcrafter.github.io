What Everhold is

Everhold helps you keep track of dates that matter: warranties, documents, renewals and more. It is a helpful reminder tool, not a legal record. Reminders depend on the dates you enter and on your iPhone's notification settings, so please do not rely on the app as your only safeguard for critical deadlines like visas or insurance cover.
