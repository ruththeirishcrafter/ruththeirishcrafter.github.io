Logline

The fast, private logbook for airline and professional pilots.

What it does

  • Import your roster once a month and your flying history keeps itself. AIMS eCrew is fully supported, and CSV or Excel exports from other crew systems import too.
  • Print a proper logbook in the EASA or FAA column layout.
  • See total hours, sectors, PIC and night, landing currency, and a route map of everywhere you have flown.
  • Track your medical, licence and recurrent checks, with reminders before they run out.
  • Everything stays on your device, kept in step across your iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.

Support

Questions, a roster that will not import, or anything else: email logline@brambleapps.com and we will get back to you. Send us a roster from a crew system we do not read yet and we will add it, and give you an extra free month.
