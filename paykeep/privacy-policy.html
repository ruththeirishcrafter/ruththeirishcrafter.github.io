Paykeep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

Your pay never reaches us. Paykeep has no account, no server and no analytics. Every payslip, bill and figure you enter is stored on your device, and in your own iCloud while syncing is on. We cannot see any of it, because it never comes to us.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no email, no tracking, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting that carries your data.

What you type in

You enter your own figures: the pay lines and the period. The app never asks for your PPS number, your employee number or your name, and it has no way to read a document, your bank or your contacts.

iCloud sync

Syncing is on to begin with, which copies your data to your own iCloud account so your other devices can read it. That account is controlled by you and Apple, and nothing passes through us. Turn it off under Settings and your data stays on the one device.

Purchases

Paykeep Pro is handled by Apple and RevenueCat. They tell us whether a purchase is active, never who you are.

Your control

Delete your data any time from Settings. Deleting the app removes everything on the device with it.
