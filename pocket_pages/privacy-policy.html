Pocket Pages — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

Pocket Pages keeps everything on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, and your notes and planner photos are never uploaded to us. The one exception is the optional iCloud backup below, which you start yourself and which goes to your own iCloud, not to us.

The optional iCloud backup

If you tap Back up to iCloud in Settings, a copy of your notes and planner pages is saved to your own private iCloud storage, so you can restore them on a new phone. It only ever happens when you ask for it. The backup sits in your Apple account, under Apple's terms; we cannot see it, and we never receive a copy.

What the app stores

Your sticky notes and planner page photos are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. They are included in your normal device backups. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Voice dictation

When you dictate a note, your iPhone's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text on the device. Pocket Pages does not record, store or upload your voice.

Purchases

If you unlock Pocket Pages Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple. A receipt check runs through RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, which sees an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase state, never your notes or photos. Nothing is linked to your identity and nothing is used for tracking or advertising. The free trial runs entirely on your device and involves no payment details.

Camera and photo library

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to photograph or add a planner page. The app never browses your library on its own.

Children

Pocket Pages is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact
